# What is Future Engineers?

The **Future Engineers** category of the **World Robot Olympiad (WRO)** challenges teams to design and program autonomous vehicles capable of reacting to real-world situations.

The competition is divided into two phases:

### Open Challenge

The robot must complete laps autonomously while maintaining stability and avoiding errors.

### Obstacle Challenge

The circuit incorporates obstacles and signs:

🟢 Green → bypass to the left  
🔴 Red → bypass to the right  

Finally, the vehicle must perform an **autonomous parking** maneuver.

# 🤖 Our Robot: "Leotardo"

Leotardo is the result of months of testing, redesigns, and improvements.

Its design was carefully developed to meet official restrictions and maximize:

- performance
- stability
- maintainability
- weight distribution
- energy efficiency

During development, it evolved from simple structures to a **3D-printed modular chassis**, allowing for higher precision and easier repairs.

---

# Main Features

Raspberry Pi + Arduino UNO  

Independent power supply for logic and power  

PLA 3D-printed modular chassis  

Multiple ultrasonic sensors  

Steering via MG996R servomotor  

High-efficiency TB6612FNG driver  

Completely modular and repairable design

# Regulations & Constraints

The robot must respect:

| Parameter | Limit |
|------------|----------|
| Size | 300×200×300 mm |
| Weight | 1.5 kg |
| Wheels | 4 required |
| Traction motors | Maximum 2 |
| Steering | Maximum 1 motor |
| Remote control | Not allowed |
